FAQs on Finding Parking Near Me

When using CarPlay, Android Auto, or voice search, ParkEase prioritizes the closest available spaces with guaranteed slot availability, real-time access clearance, and verified pricing. We factor in your current live GPS heading so the suggested spaces are along your direction of travel.

Booking on the move is built for spontaneous, live journeys. Instead of selecting specific dates and hours ahead of time, the system locks in the space immediately from the moment you tap 'Park Now' and guides you directly via your dashboard screen.

Payment is completely hands-free and cashless. Your default payment method stored in your ParkEase account (Apple Pay, Google Pay, or card) is charged automatically once you confirm the booking on your dashboard.

You can extend your active parking session with a single tap on your in-car touchscreen or via the ParkEase smartphone app before your current session expires.
