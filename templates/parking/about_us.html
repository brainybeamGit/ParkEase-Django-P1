HOW IT STARTED

From Driveway Sharing to Smart Urban Mobility

Founded with the vision that unused parking spaces can dramatically reduce traffic congestion, ParkEase began by enabling homeowners to rent out empty driveways. Today, we have evolved into India’s most comprehensive mobility ecosystem, partnering with local authorities, commercial car parks, hotel chains, and EV charging operators to deliver frictionless digital parking experiences.

Save Time & Fuel

Studies show that 30% of inner-city traffic is drivers circling for parking. Pre-booking with ParkEase gets you directly to your reserved space without circling.

Guaranteed Security

Every facility in the ParkEase network is verified by our field operations team. Secure digital QR passes and ANPR barrier automation guarantee access.

Green EV Network

We accelerate electric vehicle adoption by combining guaranteed bay reservations with fast OCPP-standard charging, creating seamless dual sessions.
