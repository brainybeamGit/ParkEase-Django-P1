Your Complete Guide to Park and Pay

NPP LOCATIONS

National Parking Platform (NPP)

You can use ParkEase to pay for parking at any NPP-enabled location. Simply enter the location number displayed on the signage to start your session. The app handles payment and timing so you don't need to use a machine. It's quick, convenient, and works anywhere NPP is in place.

COUNCIL SPACES

Council Run Car Parks

We work closely with local councils to bring seamless digital parking to council-managed car parks. Simply type the location number into the ParkEase app to begin. You can set your parking time and pay instantly on your phone. It's fast, convenient, and widely available across council sites.
