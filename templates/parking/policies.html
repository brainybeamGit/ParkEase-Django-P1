Privacy Policy

1. Introduction & Scope

Welcome to ParkEase ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal data, privacy, and vehicle details when you use our web platform, parking pre-booking systems, gate barrier passes, and owner services. This Privacy Policy outlines what information we collect, how it is processed, and your privacy choices under applicable data protection regulations.

2. Information We Collect

When you register, search, or book parking spaces on ParkEase, we collect the following categories of information:

  • Account Details: Full name, verified email address, mobile telephone number, and encrypted password credentials.
  • Vehicle Registration Information: Vehicle license registration number (e.g. DL 01 AB 1234), vehicle category (two-wheeler, hatchback, sedan, SUV, electric vehicle), make, and model.
  • Reservation & Gate Pass Logs: Parking lot location, slot assignments, entry/exit scheduled timestamps, digital QR pass authentication tokens, and gate scanner verification events.
  • Location Data: Search queries (cities, areas, airport codes) and optional browser geolocation used exclusively to compute distances and show nearby parking lots.
  • Payment Identifiers: Razorpay transaction IDs, payment status, invoice numbers, and tariff breakdown. We do not store raw credit/debit card numbers or net-banking passwords.

3. How We Use Your Information

We process your data exclusively for legitimate operational purposes:

  • Securing guaranteed parking bay reservations and generating verifiable digital QR passes.
  • Facilitating instant entry and exit authentication at parking lot gate barriers.
  • Computing transparent hourly, daily, and monthly tariffs and calculating overstay settlements when applicable.
  • Delivering real-time booking confirmation emails, digital ticket passes, and SMS receipts.
  • Enabling parking lot owners to manage space occupancy and track parking earnings.

4. Vehicle Data & License Plate Recognition

Your vehicle registration number is utilized solely to confirm authorization at the lot barrier. We never sell, rent, or trade driver vehicle records to third-party data brokers or marketing networks.

5. Payment Security

All financial transactions on ParkEase are encrypted and handled through PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant payment gateways (Razorpay). Sensitive card credentials are tokenized directly with the gateway; ParkEase servers never hold or log your confidential payment card information.

6. Cookies & Local Session Storage

We use essential session cookies and local storage tokens solely to maintain your signed-in state, remember active filter preferences (such as EV charging filters or chosen search duration), and keep your digital pass accessible across browser reloads.

7. Your Rights & Data Management

You have complete control over your information. From your Profile and My Vehicles dashboards, you can add, update, or remove registered vehicles at any time. You may also contact us to request full account closure and personal data erasure.

Privacy Inquiries: If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or your data protection rights, reach our Data Privacy desk at privacy@parkease.com.

Overstay Policy & Tariff Rules

1. Purpose & Fair Space Allocation

ParkEase operates on a guaranteed slot reservation model. When you reserve a parking slot for a specific time window, that bay is held exclusively for your vehicle. Our Overstay Policy ensures that drivers exit in a timely manner so the next scheduled driver can park without delay or obstruction.

2. 15-Minute Grace Period

We recognize that traffic, elevator wait times, or last-minute delays happen. ParkEase provides a complimentary 15-minute grace period immediately following your scheduled booking end time. If your vehicle exits the barrier within this 15-minute window, no extra tariff or overstay charge is assessed.

3. Overstay Tariff Calculation

If an exit scan occurs after the 15-minute grace period has elapsed, the system calculates the additional tariff from your original booked end time based on the parking lot's approved hourly rate:

Overstay Fee = (Elapsed Hours beyond reservation) × (Lot Hourly Tariff)

Tariffs are computed transparently. For example, if you overstay by 1.5 hours in a lot with a ₹50/hour tariff, your overstay fee is exactly ₹75.00 with zero arbitrary penalties.

4. Gate Scanner Verification & Barrier Operation

When exiting the parking facility, your digital QR pass is scanned at the exit gate:

  • On-Time Exit: The gate terminal confirms valid status, records departure timestamp, and lifts the exit barrier automatically.
  • Overstay Detected: The terminal highlights the elapsed overstay minutes, displays the calculated extra tariff, and presents an instant Razorpay settlement link. The exit barrier remains down until settlement is completed.

5. Instant Settlement & Immediate Barrier Release

Drivers can settle overstay dues in seconds using UPI, cards, or digital wallets via the prompt on their digital ticket or the gate terminal. Once settlement is confirmed, the exit barrier opens immediately.

6. Vehicle Breakdown & Emergency Overrides

If your vehicle suffers a mechanical breakdown, flat tire, or you experience an emergency, notify the on-duty lot gate operator or parking lot owner immediately. On-site staff have authorization to manually override barriers or pause billing upon verifying emergency circumstances.

7. Dispute Resolution & Refunds

If you believe an overstay charge was incorrectly calculated (for example, due to an exit gate bottleneck or scanner connectivity delay), you may submit an appeal through the Help Center with your Booking ID. Verified discrepancies are credited back within 3 to 5 business days.

Overstay Support: Have a question about an active ticket or overstay fee? Visit our Help Center or email support@parkease.com.
