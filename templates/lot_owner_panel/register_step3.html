2. Space Owner Terms & Conditions

Before listing your space on ParkEase, please review your core responsibilities as an authorized parking space owner:

  • Authorization: You have the legal right or authorization to offer the parking space for rental.
  • Accuracy: You will maintain accurate availability, pricing, operating hours, and clear entrance guidelines.
  • Honoring Bookings: You will honor confirmed bookings and follow ParkEase standard host cancellation policies.

ParkEase Space Owner Terms & Conditions
1. Listing Eligibility & Property Authority

By registering as a Space Owner on ParkEase, you warrant that you are the legal owner, designated property manager, or lawful tenant authorized in writing by the property owner to sublet or license vehicular parking at the designated location.

2. Operating Hours & Real-Time Availability

You agree to keep your operating schedule, gate access instructions, and bay availability up to date. If your space becomes temporarily unavailable due to private events or maintenance, you must update your calendar promptly to prevent unfulfillable bookings.

3. Booking Fulfillment & Cancellation

Space Owners must honor all confirmed bookings accepted through the platform. Unwarranted cancellations or turning away drivers with valid digital parking passes undermines platform integrity and will incur penalty charges or lead to account de-listing.

4. Fee Schedule & Payout Settlement

ParkEase charges a transparent service commission on each completed reservation. Host payouts are disbursed on a scheduled cycle directly to the registered bank account after booking completion, net of applicable fees and taxes.

5. Maintenance, Safety & Liability

You are responsible for maintaining the physical accessibility and baseline safety of the parking space. Any hazardous conditions or property changes must be reported immediately to ParkEase Support.

ParkEase Space Owner Privacy Policy
1. Information We Collect

We collect your full legal name, contact email, mobile phone number, residential or business address, and government-issued identity documents (such as Aadhaar, PAN, Passport, Driving Licence, or Voter ID) along with physical photos of your parking bay.

2. Why We Collect This Data

Identity verification is legally mandated to protect both homeowners and drivers against fraud, verify property rights, comply with local municipal security norms, and enable automated direct payout settlements.

3. Storage & Encryption

All sensitive documents and identity credentials are stored on secure, encrypted servers. Drivers only see your space address, landmark, and entrance instructions once their reservation is confirmed; they never see your private ID credentials.

4. Data Sharing & Retention

We do not sell your personal information. Information is retained only as long as your account remains active and as required by tax and accounting legislation. You may request account closure or data erasure in accordance with applicable laws.
